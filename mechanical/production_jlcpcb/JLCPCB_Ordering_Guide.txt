1. UPLOAD INSTRUCTIONS (JLCPCB 3D Printing):
   Go to: https://jlcpcb.com/3d-printing
   
   Upload the two parts individually (either STEP or STL format):
   - Part 1: 01_Base_Tray.step (or 01_Base_Tray.stl)
   - Part 2: 02_Upper_Hood.step (or 02_Upper_Hood.stl)

2. RECOMMENDED MANUFACTURING SPECIFICATIONS:

   [Option A - High Aesthetic Desktop Finish (Recommended)]:
   - Technology: SLA (Stereolithography)
   - Material  : 8000 White Resin (or 8001 / Black Resin / Imagine Black)
   - Color     : White or Black
   - Layer H   : 0.1 mm standard (or 0.05 mm fine)
   - Infill    : 100% Solid
   - Surface   : Clean / Sanded (Matte)

   [Option B - Heavy Duty Workshop / Industrial]:
   - Technology: SLS / MJF
   - Material  : PA12-HP Nylon (Natural Grey / Dyed Black)
   - Infill    : Solid

3. PART DIMENSIONS & METRICS:
   - Part 1: Base_Tray  -> 320.0 x 185.0 x 38.0 mm
   - Part 2: Upper_Hood -> 320.0 x 185.0 x 70.0 mm
   - Wall Thickness     : 3.0 mm uniform
   - Screw Bosses       : Optimized lightweight ribbed bosses (M3, dia 2.5mm pre-hole)

4. HARDWARE FASTENERS REQUIRED:
   - 5x M3 x 16mm or M3 x 20mm Countersunk (Flat Head) Screws for Case Assembly
   - 6x M3 x 6mm or M3 x 8mm Pan Head / Self-tapping Screws for Mainboard Standoffs (H1..H7)
   - 4x M3 x 6mm Pan Head Screws for Mean Well PSU Standoffs
